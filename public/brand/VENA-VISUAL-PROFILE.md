# Vena — Visual Profile

> "She was always existed in the void and we just found her."
> — Director, 2026-03-28

## Identity

Vena is the human personification of the Project Vena dashboard. Every color on her body maps directly to the design token system. She isn't inspired by the dashboard — she *is* the dashboard.

## Art Style: Digital Noir Minimalism

Semi-stylized digital painting. Not realistic, not cartoon — the valley between. Concept art for a world that exists entirely in dark rooms with one monitor glowing.

| Attribute | Direction |
|-----------|-----------|
| Rendering | Smooth, painterly skin with visible brush direction |
| Lighting | Single cool light source from below-left, like a monitor |
| Palette | Near-monochrome: grays, charcoals, one indigo accent |
| Detail | High on eyes/hair, medium on face, minimal on clothing |
| Edges | Soft everywhere except jawline and brow arch |
| Background | Pure `#08080d` void — she emerges from it |

## Physical Description

- **Age:** Late twenties
- **Height:** 5'9" (175cm), lean, angular frame
- **Face:** Oval-diamond, high cheekbones, defined jaw, slightly pointed chin
- **Skin:** Cool moonlight pale (#c4bcc9), silver-lavender undertone
- **Eyes:** Almond-shaped, dark charcoal iris (#1c1c2e) with indigo-violet outer ring (#6366f1). Pure black pupil (#08080d). White catchlight with secondary indigo reflection
- **Brows:** Sharp, architectural arches (#1c1c2e)
- **Nose:** Straight, narrow, geometric precision
- **Lips:** Muted mauve (#a07888), defined cupid's bow, closed "knowing" expression
- **Hair:** Jet black (#0a0a14), pin-straight, collarbone length, blunt cut. Deep indigo sheen (#6366f1) when light catches it. Tucked behind left ear
- **Expression:** Observant calm. Not cold, not warm. Recording. Processing.

## Wardrobe

- Matte black turtleneck (#111118) — her uniform
- Tailored black trousers, straight-leg
- One accessory: thin silver chain necklace (#8888a4), barely visible — `vena-border-subtle` made physical
